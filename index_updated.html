A Message for You 💌

Happy monthsary, my love. ♡

June 10, 2023 — the day we became us. And now, here we are, celebrating 3 years and 4 months together. Sometimes I think about how many little moments, lessons, laughs, and challenges have brought us here. I may not always find the perfect words, but I hope you always feel how much you mean to me.

One of the things I love most about you is the goodness in your heart. I still think about August 28, 2026, when you initiated giving something to someone outside the 7-Eleven in front of PJG. It might have looked like a simple gesture to someone else, but to me, it meant so much. Seeing you genuinely want to help another person made my heart feel full. It reminded me that kindness is not just something you say you believe in — it is something you choose to do.

You know how much it means to me when I can help someone, even in a small way. There are things I wish I could have given my lolo and lola when I was younger, when I wasn't yet capable of giving them everything they wanted. Maybe that's one reason why helping people touches me so deeply. So when I saw that same softness and willingness to give in you, I felt something beyond kilig. I felt grateful. I thought, tamang tao yung napili ko.

I love you for the way you make me feel, but I also love the person you are when no one is asking you to prove anything. I love that your heart can make room for someone else's struggle. I hope that as we grow together, we keep that part of ourselves alive — that we become more patient, more understanding, and more capable of giving, even when life isn't always easy.

Of course, we're not perfect. We'll have misunderstandings, hard days, and moments when we need to learn how to understand each other better. But I hope we keep choosing to listen, to grow, and to meet each other with love. I don't expect us to have everything figured out. I just want us to keep trying, cheering for each other's dreams, and building a relationship that feels safe, honest, and kind.

Thank you for being part of my life, for the little things you do, and for the memories we're still making. Thank you for reminding me that love isn't only about grand gestures or what we can give each other. Sometimes, it's also about the kind of people we become because we love each other.

Happy 40th monthsary, baby. From June 10, 2023, to this day, I'm thankful for our story. I hope we get to celebrate many more dates, reach the dreams we talk about, and have enough to share kindness with the people who need it. Whatever the future brings, I hope we never forget to be gentle with each other and with the world around us.

If I had to choose all over again, I'd still choose you — not because everything is always easy, but because there is so much about your heart that I admire and so much about us that I want to keep growing.

Officially yours, and still choosing you. Always. ♡

With all my love, your girl ♡
